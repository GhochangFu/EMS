# ADR 0053 — E5.2 mechanical/utility pack: provisional authoring from a derived tag list

`build-operating-model.md` step 2 gate.** Drafted the same morning `E5.1`
closed (PR #293, #294, #295), for the owner's "Start E5.2", and put to the

**Built by `E5.2` on 2026-09-03 (PR #297, squash `e13285d`)** — six provisional
machine-class templates in the ruled order under two domains, the `mechanical`
domain seeded, 107 new point keys, all eleven decisions as accepted with the

**Prerequisites, all met:** `E5.2`'s three `Depends` entries — `F2.1`
(ADR 0015), `E1.7` (ADR 0019) and `F2.13` (ADR 0052) — are ✅. ADR 0019
Amendment 2 (`thresholdValue`/`operator` paired optional) was accepted
2026-09-02 for `E5.1` and is what lets this pack carry alarm meanings without
numbers. ADR 0040's eight decisions, as corrected at `E5.1`'s closure, are the
shape this pack inherits; this ADR restates only what differs or was not

**This is the tag-list citation `docs/BACKLOG.md` §5 requires** before any
`E5.2` template content is built: the v1 point basis for the mechanical/utility
pack is [`docs/e5.2-derived-taglist-v1.md`](../e5.2-derived-taglist-v1.md),
PROVISIONAL, derived from ISA-5.1, ASHRAE Guideline 36-2021, Project Haystack,
the controller register maps that ship on the equipment (Atlas Copco
Elektronikon, ABB ACS580), ISO 20816 / ANSI/HI 9.6.4, IBR log-sheet practice
and the repository's existing `HVAC_POINT_KEYS` — not from a client export.
Every entry's `description` cites the file and its section, and `checkEntry`'s
`PACK_SOURCE_DOC` map fails the build for a pack that does not.

## Context

`E5.2` is the next head of Track B. The 2026-08-22 client sheet (row 4) names
**chillers, compressors and HVAC** explicitly, asks for chiller-health
analytics (form ref **N5**), and its framing is building-wide; form ref **N4**
commits us to authoring KPI library v1 ourselves — chiller kW/TR and COP,
cooling-tower approach/range, availability — which means the points those
KPIs need must exist in templates before the KPIs can. Ion Exchange's real tag
list (form ref **A1**) has still not landed, and ADR 0040 already replaced the
wait-for-client posture with a review-our-draft posture across Track B: derive,
tier, version, redline.

What the derived tag list holds, measured on 2026-09-03: **128 point rows over
six classes** — pump set (18), motor + VFD (15), air compressor (21), chiller
(25), AHU (26), boiler (23) — **115 distinct codes**, 54 core / 69 extended /
4 manual / 1 dual-tier (`feedwater_tds_ppm`, `X/M`). **20 of the 115 codes are
already in the global vocabulary** (`current_a`, `kw`, `kwh_total`,
`run_hours_h`, `start_count`, `winding_temp_c`, `insulation_resistance_mohm`,
`oil_temp_c`, `oil_pressure_bar`, `service_due_h`, the seven `HVAC_POINT_KEYS`
the document reuses by name, `fan_current_a`), every one with a compatible
unit; **95 are new** (94 once the plan's ruling 1 reused the DG set's
`fuel_level_pct` for the boiler's day tank — see the correction under decision
3). The eight codes that appear in more than one class are all
among the 20 already seeded, so the new codes partition cleanly between the
classes. The cooling tower is **referenced, not repeated**: `water-cooling-tower`
(`E5.1`) is the tower, and the document says so.

Two things the water pack did not have to decide:

1. **The domain.** `bms.asset_domains` holds `electrical`, `hvac`, `it`,
   `environment`, `water` (migration `0029`) — there is no `mechanical`. A
   chiller and an AHU are HVAC in every taxonomy the tag list draws on (Haystack,
   G36) and in this repository (`HVAC_POINT_KEYS` already carries their chilled-
   water and air-side keys under `hvac`); a pump set, a VFD, an air compressor
   and a boiler are not. ADR 0031 Amendment 1 made the domain a dynamic
   vocabulary precisely so a pack could add one: *"declaring a value is an
   `INSERT` a domain pack ships in its own seed."* This pack is the first to do
   it.
2. **Composition.** The document's own cross-cutting note is that classes
   compose — *"a CHW primary pump is §1 + §2; a chiller plant is n × §4 + n × §1
   + E5.1 §4 towers"* — and that templates carry the base class while the asset
   group carries the composition (ADR 0031: domain from the asset, concern from
   the rule). ADR 0040's ruling 5 (one asset per plant, plant assets in a group
   at their location) is the same shape one level down.

## Decision

1. **Author the mechanical/utility pack as six stock catalog entries at
   `stockVersion: 1`** (ADR 0052), marked provisional, transcribed from the tag
   list in **document order: pump set → motor + VFD → air compressor → chiller →
   AHU → boiler.** Document order is the order the handout reads in, and the
   pump set is the base class the document says every other pack's pump is.
   The cooling tower is not forked; a chiller plant composes `water-cooling-tower`.
2. **Two domains, and the code prefix says which.** The chiller and the AHU are
   **`hvac`** — the existing domain whose vocabulary already holds their keys.
   The pump set, the VFD, the air compressor and the boiler are **`mechanical`**,
   a new `bms.asset_domains` row (`code = 'mechanical'`, `label = 'Mechanical'`,
   `sort_order = 60`) landed as an idempotent `INSERT` through the vocabulary
   seed path, per ADR 0031 A1.1 — the first domain added since `0029`. Entry
   codes keep the convention both shipped packs set, prefix = domain:
   `mechanical-pump`, `mechanical-vfd`, `mechanical-compressor`, `hvac-chiller`,
   `hvac-ahu`, `mechanical-boiler`, with `assetType` `pump`, `vfd`,
   `air_compressor`, `chiller`, `ahu`, `boiler`. `PACK_SOURCE_DOC` gains two
   prefixes, `hvac` and `mechanical`, both citing the `E5.2` file. (Gate
   question 1 offers the one-domain alternative.)
3. **Vocabulary through ADR 0051's seed path, existing keys reused verbatim.**
   The 95 new codes land as two arrays in `packages/shared/src/constants.ts` —
   the chiller's and the AHU's under `hvac`, the other four classes' under
   `mechanical` — with `UNIT_BY_KEY` entries and `keysForDomain(…)` lines
   appended after the water pack's, in document order, first occurrence wins.
   The 20 already-seeded codes are **referenced, never redeclared**: units are
   write-once, so `fan_rpm` stays `RPM`, and `start_count` / `compressor_ok`
   stay the empty string. Unit spellings follow the document, with the rulings
   `E5.1` already took: `0/1`, `enum`, `code` and `count` rows map to the empty
   string (ADR 0051 Amendment 6 decision 4); `KL/hr` and `pH` as spelled;
   `°C` is `U+00B0`, `MΩ` and `m³/hr` keep their existing Unicode. `bms.point_keys`
   goes **291 → 386** on a cold start, no migration.

   *Corrected at `E5.2`'s closure (2026-09-03, PR #297): the arrays hold **107**
   codes and `bms.point_keys` went **291 → 398**, not 95 and 386. Two things the
   draft did not count: the thirteen derived codes decision 6 promotes are
   vocabulary too — a derived template point's `pointKey` must pass
   `assertPointKeysActive`, so it is seeded like any measured key (ADR 0051
   Amendment 6 decision 8, the water pack's seven were counted the same way) —
   and the plan's §12 ruling 1 reused the DG set's `fuel_level_pct` for the
   boiler's day-tank level, so 94 table codes, not 95. 94 + 13 = 107; 291 + 107 =
   398. `MECHANICAL_CLASS_POINT_KEYS` is 68 and `HVAC_CLASS_POINT_KEYS` 39. The
   rest of the decision held: two arrays, existing keys referenced never
   redeclared, the units as spelled.*
4. **Tiering on the data** (ADR 0040 decision 3, ruled `meta.tier`, no DDL):
   `C` → `core` / required, `X` → `extended` / optional, `M` → `manual` /
   optional via `F1.8` / `F1.9`. The one dual-tier row resolves first-listed-wins,
   as the water pack's two did: `feedwater_tds_ppm` is `extended` on the boiler.
5. **Alarms are pair-absent philosophy rows** (ADR 0019 Amendment 2) —
   parameter, meaning, severity, category, `philosophy {cause, impact, action,
   skill?}` — **no threshold numbers**, including the IBR-critical drum-level
   pair and the safety-valve-lift pressure. `philosophy.skill` is set where one
   of `0034`'s five trades genuinely answers (`mechanical`, `hvac`, `electrical`,
   `controls`) and **omitted on the process-chemistry rows** (boiler water pH,
   feedwater and blowdown TDS, flue-gas O₂/CO), exactly as `E5.1` ruling 6 did;
   `F4.78` files the `process` trade. Vendor fault codes are carried in the
   alarm text, never enumerated — v2 fixes the enum per OEM controller.
6. **Derived points, not `content.kpis`** (ADR 0040 decision 5 as corrected at
   `E5.1`'s closure): every single-asset ratio the document names that is
   expressible with `bms-calc-v1` over the entry's own declared points is a
   **derived template point** an alarm can bind; every other named derived code
   is **deferred and named** in the entry docblock, never placeholdered (ADR 0051
   Amendment 6 decision 8). The N4 chiller KPIs are the first candidates for
   promotion because every input is core — `cooling_load_tr`, `kw_per_tr`,
   `cop`, `chw_delta_t_c`, `cw_delta_t_c` — and the pump's `head_m`, the
   compressor's `load_factor_pct` and the AHU's `sat_deviation_c` follow; the
   ones that need a time window (`starts_per_hour`, `availability_pct`,
   `approach_trend`, `unload_cycles_per_hour`), an asset attribute
   (`motor_load_pct`, `part_load_pct`), or a method the document only names
   (`efficiency_indirect_pct`, `air_leak_estimate_pct`, `vibration_band`) are
   deferred. The plan settles the exact promoted set and records the
   reconciliation (promoted + deferred = named).
7. **Provenance is the stock stamp plus the citation** (ADR 0052 decision 6,
   ADR 0040 decision 6 as corrected): `stock_code` / `stock_version` on the
   imported row and a `description` that cites the tag-list file and section.
   The `A1` redline produces `stockVersion: 2`; ADR 0039 governs how it reaches
   instantiated assets; nothing instantiated is rewritten.
8. **Maintenance plans are authored, PROVISIONAL** (`E5.1` ruling 5 applied to
   the same gap), derived from the OEM, ISO 20816 and IBR practice the document
   cites, with `safetyCritical` where the trade says so — the boiler's
   low-water cut-off and safety-valve test, the compressor's pressure-relief
   test, the AHU's fire-trip interlock test. The plan names each.
9. **One asset per machine; composition is the asset group's.** The template is
   the base class. A driven load with its VFD, a compressor house, a chiller
   plant are compositions of assets in a group at their location, not
   templates. **The VFD is therefore its own template and its own asset**, on
   the fieldbus register block the document transcribes, attached to what it
   drives by the group. A parent-child train is a v2 shape behind `F2.10`.
   (Gate question 3 offers the fold-into-the-pump alternative.)
10. **Names stay generic.** Template names are machine-class names; OEM and
    IESL product names swap in as display names when confirmed.
11. **Scope fence.** This ADR authors *content* — vocabulary, entries, alarm
    philosophy rows, derived formulas, maintenance plans — plus one vocabulary
    row. It does **not** build chiller-health analytics (`N5`: `kw_per_tr` is a
    derived point and *"kW/TR high vs baseline"* is a pair-absent alarm meaning;
    the health signal is ADR 0050's surface, not this row's), does not wire
    alarms to rules (`E2.4`), does not build or rebind dashboards (`F3.1`,
    `F3.45`), does not touch the hierarchy (`F2.10`), does not add the `process`
    skill (`F4.78`), the runtime import parse (`F2.16`) or the catalog accordion
    (`F2.17`), and does not rename or retire the seeded `BASELINE-HVAC` template
    or change `apps/sim` — the reused `HVAC_POINT_KEYS` are the reason both keep
    working unchanged.

## Dependencies

**None.** No npm package. No DDL: the `mechanical` domain is one `INSERT` into
an existing lookup table through the seed path ADR 0031 A1.1 prescribes, and
the 95 point keys ride ADR 0051's seed exactly as the water pack's 98 did.
AGENTS.md §9.4 is not engaged. `packages/db` is touched (the seed), so
`migration-reviewer` joins the review.

## Consequences

- **The catalog grows 12 → 18 entries**, with the first two entries under a
  domain the accordion `F2.17` will group as "HVAC" beside the org's own
  `BASELINE-HVAC` template. That coexistence is deliberate: the baseline is an
  organization's own template (no stock stamp), the stock chiller and AHU are
  catalog content, and an org that imports the stock chiller gets a second,
  provisional, richer template — not a replacement.
- **`stock-catalog.spec.ts` is at 978 lines** and the third and fourth
  `PACK_SOURCE_DOC` prefixes plus the seventh `DEFERRED_DERIVED_CODES` list
  cross AGENTS.md §4.5's cap, so the plan takes the split as Task 1
  (`E5.1` plan §13 item 12). `UNKNOWN_PACK_FIXTURE` is spelled
  `mechanical-chiller` today *because* `mechanical` is not a pack; the fixture
  moves to a prefix that stays undeclared.
- **Guard bounds move to their measured actuals**, never left slack:
  `tests/f2.13` (391 / 266 / 289 and `STOCK_ASSET_RELS` 16), `tests/f3.38`
  (289), `tests/f3.39` (289 / 9) — each to whatever the build measures at
  `386` keys and 18 entries. *Measured: 398 keys (decision 3's correction), and
  the bounds landed at `f2.13` 584 / 382 / 396 with `STOCK_ASSET_RELS` 23,
  `f3.38` 396, `f3.39` 396 / 11.*
- **Form ref N4 is partially discharged** for chillers by decision 6 — kW/TR
  and COP exist as derived points the client can review — and stays open for
  availability (a time-window quantity the calc grammar cannot express) and for
  kWh/KL (cross-asset, `F2.9`). Both client-facing statements — Part 1 of the
  2026-08-22 form and the reply mail — already say authoring proceeds pack by
  pack; nothing there needs correcting for this ADR.
- **The exposure is the extended tier** (69 of 128 rows, mostly condition-
  monitoring sensors — bearing RTDs, vibration, flue O₂ — and controller
  extras) and the OEM fault-code enums, both correctable by one redline session
  the workshop handout already requests.
- On acceptance, `E5.2`'s BACKLOG row flips to 🟡 and §5's *Tag-list citation*
  row records `E5.2` as cited — a `docs(backlog):` change on the feature branch,
  the way `E5.1`'s was; the `chore(agents):` sweep at closure is separate (§9.10).

## Open questions for the gate — all three answered 2026-09-03

1. **Two domains or one?** Drafted (decision 2) as `hvac` for the chiller and
   the AHU plus a new `mechanical` for the other four, prefix = domain. The
   alternative is one `mechanical` domain for all six with one `mechanical-`
   prefix — simpler to count, but it files a chiller under a domain other than
   the one its own keys are seeded in, and it puts the stock chiller in a
   different accordion group from the org's `BASELINE-HVAC` chiller.
   **Ruled: two domains, as drafted.**
2. **All six classes in v1, in document order?** The client's sheet names three
   (chiller, compressor, HVAC); the pump set, the VFD and the boiler come from
   the document's own framing (the pump is the base class every other pack's
   pump is; the boiler is the IBR log sheet an Indian plant already keeps).
   Including them is a scope statement the same way ETP's inclusion was for
   `E5.1` — better ruled than drifted. **Ruled: all six, in document order.**
   The alternatives offered — the client's three first, or the three only —
   were declined: the handout and the catalog read in the same order.
3. **The VFD as its own template and asset?** Drafted (decision 9) as a
   standalone `mechanical-vfd` composed by the asset group, per the document's
   cross-cutting note. The alternative folds the fifteen drive points into the
   pump and compressor entries as extended rows — fewer assets to create, but
   the same register block transcribed twice, and no way to template a VFD on a
   fan or a blower this pack does not carry. **Ruled: its own template.**
