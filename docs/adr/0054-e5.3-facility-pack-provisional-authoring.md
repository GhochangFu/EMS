# ADR 0054 — E5.3 facility/smart-building pack: provisional authoring from a derived tag list

## Status

**Accepted — 2026-09-03, by the repository owner, at the
`build-operating-model.md` step 2 gate.** Drafted the same day `E5.2` closed
(PR #297, #298, #299), for the owner's "Start E5.3", and put to the owner one
question at a time. **All three questions at the end were ruled as drafted**;
the rulings are recorded at the foot of this document.

**Prerequisites, all met:** `E5.3`'s three `Depends` entries — `F2.1`
(ADR 0015), `E1.7` (ADR 0019) and `F2.13` (ADR 0052) — are ✅. ADR 0019
Amendment 2 (`thresholdValue`/`operator` paired optional) is what lets this
pack carry alarm meanings without numbers. ADR 0040's eight decisions as
corrected at `E5.1`'s closure, and ADR 0053's eleven as corrected at `E5.2`'s,
are the shape this pack inherits; this ADR restates only what differs or was
not decided there.

**On the wave.** `E5.3` is a **wave 5, P2** row and the board says so — *"
Unblocked, but its wave is far out."* It is being built now because the owner
started it, not because the wave arrived, and the board's own critical-path
note already records it as the next head of Track B after `E5.2`. Nothing in
this ADR re-prices the wave; the §1 wave plan is unchanged.

**This is the tag-list citation `docs/BACKLOG.md` §5 requires** before any
`E5.3` template content is built: the v1 point basis for the facility/smart-
building pack is [`docs/e5.3-derived-taglist-v1.md`](../e5.3-derived-taglist-v1.md),
PROVISIONAL, derived from ISA-5.1, BACnet standard objects (the Life Safety
Point / Life Safety Zone objects and the SSPC-135 *Elevator Group / Lift /
Escalator* objects), NFPA 72 / IS 2189 event classes, DALI-2 / IEC 62386 parts
252, 253, 303 and 304, the ISHRAE IEQ Standard and ASHRAE 62.1, EN 81-20/50,
EN 115 / IS 4591, the OEM lift telemetry APIs (KONE, Otis, Schindler, TK) and
the repository's own `CONTROL_ROOM_ENVIRONMENT_POINT_KEYS` — not from a client
export. Every entry's `description` cites the file and its section, and
`checkEntry`'s `PACK_SOURCE_DOC` map fails the build for a pack that does not.

## Context

`E5.3` is the last of the three domain packs. Its classes are the ones a
building has that a plant does not: the subsystems that sit on a BAS and speak
BACnet, Modbus or a vendor gateway rather than on a process P&ID. The
2026-08-22 client sheet's use-case header names **hotels, data centres and
townships**; the SOW page-10 footer shows **Data Quality 98.6% Good**, a number
that comes from the gateway-health class this pack authors; and the repository
already ships a control-room environment class whose four keys the IAQ node
reuses verbatim.

What the derived tag list holds, measured on 2026-09-03 against
`packages/shared/src/constants.ts` at `main` `0c86d80`: **227 point rows over
nine classes** — lighting zone (15), fire alarm panel (24), access control
(16), occupancy and space (10), parking (16), IAQ node (15), BAS gateway (13),
lift (78), escalator (40) — **210 distinct codes**, 40 core / 159 extended /
8 manual / 3 dual-tier. **11 of the 210 are already in the global vocabulary**
— `smoke_state`, `leak_state`, `temperature_c`, `humidity_pct` from
`CONTROL_ROOM_ENVIRONMENT_POINT_KEYS`; `motor_current_a`, `motor_temp_c` and
`vibration_mms` from `E5.2`'s `MECHANICAL_CLASS_POINT_KEYS`; `kw`,
`kwh_total`, `run_hours_h` and `start_count` from the electrical arrays —
**and every one of the eleven carries a compatible unit** (checked by script
against `UNIT_BY_KEY`, not by eye: the `0/1` and `count` rows map to the empty
string exactly as ADR 0051 Amendment 6 decision 4 rules, and `°C`, `%`, `A`,
`kW`, `kWh`, `h` and `mm/s` match as spelled). **199 are new.** Sixteen codes
appear in more than one class, and the multi-class set is where the domain
question bites: `co_ppm` and `sensor_battery_pct` cross the facility/environment
line, and `controller_comms_ok` crosses facility/mechanical.

Three things the earlier two packs did not have to decide:

1. **Three domains, not one or two.** `bms.asset_domains` holds `electrical`,
   `hvac`, `it`, `environment`, `water` (migration `0029`) and `mechanical`
   (`E5.2`'s seed row). Six of the nine classes fit none of them and need a new
   `facility` row. But an **IAQ node is the control-room environment class one
   floor up** — it reuses `temperature_c` and `humidity_pct` by name and adds
   the pollutants that class never carried — and a **lift and an escalator are
   `mechanical` machines** whose motor, brake, drive and energy points already
   collide with `E5.2`'s. Filing either under `facility` would put a code in a
   domain other than the one its own siblings are seeded in, which is the exact
   argument ADR 0053 decision 2 used to keep the chiller under `hvac`.
2. **Half the pack is one subsystem.** §8a and §8b are 118 of the 227 rows,
   more than `E5.2`'s entire pack. The tag list gives them their own section
   and their own two tables precisely because vertical transport is a different
   procurement, a different trade and a different data source from the BAS.
3. **The extended tier means something different here.** The tag list says so:
   for a plant instrument `X` means "depends on a sensor being fitted"; for a
   facility subsystem it means "depends on which gateway or protocol the site
   has". A dry-contact lift gives three to six binaries; the same lift on a
   KONE API gives seventy. 159 of 210 codes are `X` for that reason, and the
   ratio is a property of the domain, not a drafting looseness.

## Decision

1. **Author the facility/smart-building pack as nine stock catalog entries at
   `stockVersion: 1`** (ADR 0052), marked provisional, transcribed from the tag
   list in **document order: lighting zone → fire alarm panel → access control
   → occupancy and space → parking → IAQ node → BAS gateway → lift →
   escalator.** Document order is the order the handout reads in, and it is the
   order `GET /stock` returns and the `F2.17` card renders.
2. **Three domains, and the code prefix says which.** Six classes are
   **`facility`**, a new `bms.asset_domains` row (`code = 'facility'`,
   `label = 'Facility'`, `sort_order = 70`) landed as an idempotent `INSERT`
   through the vocabulary seed path ADR 0031 A1.1 prescribes — the second
   domain added since `0029`, and the module `E5.2` wrote for exactly this
   (`packages/db/src/asset-domains-seed.ts` names `facility` in its docblock).
   The IAQ node is **`environment`**, the existing domain whose vocabulary
   already holds its temperature and humidity keys. The lift and the escalator
   are **`mechanical`**, `E5.2`'s domain, whose vocabulary already holds seven
   of their codes. Entry codes keep the convention all three shipped packs set,
   prefix = domain: `facility-lighting-zone`, `facility-fire-panel`,
   `facility-access-door`, `facility-occupancy-zone`, `facility-parking-level`,
   `environment-iaq-node`, `facility-bas-gateway`, `mechanical-lift`,
   `mechanical-escalator`, with `assetType` `lighting_zone`, `fire_panel`,
   `access_door`, `occupancy_zone`, `parking_level`, `iaq_node`,
   `bas_gateway`, `lift`, `escalator`. **`PACK_SOURCE_DOC` must stop keying on
   the prefix, and this decision is what forces it**: the map is
   `Record<prefix, document>` today and already holds
   `mechanical: "e5.2-derived-taglist-v1.md"`, so a `mechanical-lift` entry
   would be required to cite `E5.2`'s document — a citation check that passes
   while pointing at the wrong source is worse than none. The map takes an
   entry-code key, or a per-entry override over the prefix default; the plan
   designs it and PR 2 is where it lands, PR 1 needing only the new `facility`
   and `environment` prefixes. `E5.2`'s own comment already named this axis
   — *"the prefix names the DOMAIN, the value names the SOURCE, and the two are
   not the same axis"* — and `E5.3` is the pack where they stop coinciding.
   (Gate question 1 offers the one-domain alternative.)
3. **Vocabulary through ADR 0051's seed path, existing keys reused verbatim.**
   The 199 new table codes land as three arrays in
   `packages/shared/src/constants.ts` — `FACILITY_CLASS_POINT_KEYS` (89),
   `ENVIRONMENT_CLASS_POINT_KEYS` (11) and an extension of the vertical-
   transport codes (99) — with `UNIT_BY_KEY` entries and `keysForDomain(…)`
   lines appended after the mechanical pack's, in document order, **first
   occurrence wins**, as ADR 0053 decision 3 rules. That rule settles the three
   cross-domain codes without a further decision: `co_ppm` and
   `sensor_battery_pct` are declared once under **`facility`** (§4 and §5
   precede §6), and `controller_comms_ok` once under **`facility`** (§3
   precedes §8). A point key names a measurement, not an estate — ADR 0051
   decision 2 — so one declaration is correct and the second class references
   it. The 11 already-seeded codes are **referenced, never redeclared**: units
   are write-once, so `temperature_c` stays `°C` and `start_count` stays the
   empty string. Unit spellings follow the document, with the rulings the
   earlier packs took: `0/1`, `enum`, `code`, `count` and `text` rows map to
   the empty string (ADR 0051 Amendment 6 decision 4), which is 141 of the 227
   rows; `µg/m³` uses `U+00B5` MICRO SIGN, matching `E5.1`'s `µS/cm`, and
   `m³` keeps the existing `U+00B3`; `dB(A)`, `CFU/m³`, `ppb`, `lux`, `m/s`,
   `m/s²`, `m/s³` and `mm` as spelled. `floor` and `date` are **not** units and
   map to the empty string; the plan records them. `bms.point_keys` goes
   **398 → 597** on a cold start before derived promotions, no migration.
4. **Tiering on the data** (ADR 0040 decision 3, ruled `meta.tier`, no DDL):
   `C` → `core` / required, `X` → `extended` / optional, `M` → `manual` /
   optional via `F1.8` / `F1.9`. The three dual-tier rows resolve
   first-listed-wins, as `E5.1`'s two and `E5.2`'s one did: `co_ppm` is
   **`core`** (§5 parking, where it is the ventilation interlock), and the two
   `X/D` rows — `entrapment_state` (§8a) and `handrail_speed_dev_pct` (§8b) —
   are **`extended`** points that are *also* derived, the shape `E5.2`'s
   `head_m` already has.
5. **Alarms are pair-absent philosophy rows** (ADR 0019 Amendment 2) —
   parameter, meaning, severity, category, `philosophy {cause, impact, action,
   skill?}` — **no threshold numbers**, including the EN 115 handrail-speed
   band and the ISHRAE IEQ limits. `philosophy.skill` is set where one of
   `0034`'s five trades genuinely answers (`electrical`, `controls`,
   `mechanical`, `hvac`) and **omitted where none does** — the fire, access and
   occupancy rows, whose trade is life-safety or security, exactly as `E5.1`
   ruling 6 and `E5.2` decision 5 omitted it on process chemistry; `F4.78`
   already files the missing-trade gap. **The fire and lift alarm rows carry
   the strongest safety meaning in the catalog and still carry no numbers**,
   because the panel owns the setpoint and the BMS observes. Vendor fault codes
   are carried in the alarm text, never enumerated.
6. **Derived points, not `content.kpis`** (ADR 0040 decision 5 as corrected):
   every single-asset ratio the document names that is expressible with
   `bms-calc-v1` over the entry's own declared points is a **derived template
   point** an alarm can bind; every other named derived code is **deferred and
   named** in the entry docblock, never placeholdered (ADR 0051 Amendment 6
   decision 8). The document names **37 derived codes**. The promotable
   candidates are the instantaneous ratios — `lighting_w_per_m2` (needs an area
   attribute, so probably deferred), `co2_above_outdoor_ppm`,
   `handrail_speed_dev_pct`, `levelling_drift_mm`, `denied_ratio_pct` — and the
   large deferred class is everything needing a **time window**
   (`lit_while_unoccupied_min_day`, `override_hours_day`, `uptime_pct`,
   `availability_pct`, `mtbf_h`, `mttr_h`, `trips_per_day`, `turnover_per_day`,
   `starts_per_day`, `peak_hour_wait_s`, `hours_out_of_band_day`), an **asset
   attribute** (`occupancy_pct`, `space_utilization_pct`), or a **method the
   document only names** (`iaq_index`, `ride_quality_index`,
   `ventilation_adequacy_pct`, `data_quality_pct`). The plan settles the exact
   promoted set and records the reconciliation (promoted + deferred = named).
   **`data_quality_pct` is deferred, not built** — the SOW footer number is
   `F3.x` surface work over the whole estate, not a per-gateway template point.
7. **Provenance is the stock stamp plus the citation** (ADR 0052 decision 6):
   `stock_code` / `stock_version` on the imported row and a `description` that
   cites the tag-list file and section. A real BAS, fire or lighting export
   produces `stockVersion: 2`; ADR 0039 governs how it reaches instantiated
   assets; nothing instantiated is rewritten.
8. **Maintenance plans are authored, PROVISIONAL** (`E5.1` ruling 5 applied to
   the same gap), derived from NFPA 72, IS 2189, EN 81-20/50, EN 115 / IS 4591
   and DALI-2 emergency-test practice, with `safetyCritical` where the trade
   says so — the fire panel's battery and detector tests, the emergency-lighting
   duration test, the lift's brake and overspeed-governor tests and its annual
   statutory inspection, the escalator's comb-plate, step-chain and
   safety-circuit checks. The plan names each.
9. **Nine entries; the lift and the escalator do not merge.** ADR 0053 decision
   9 ruled the VFD its own template on the same reasoning, and the two point
   sets diverge sharply — a lift has car position, door cycles, levelling and
   ride quality; an escalator has handrail speed, step chain and comb plates.
   They share ten codes, declared once and referenced twice. One asset per
   machine; a lift group or an escalator bank is an asset group at its
   location, not a template. (Gate question 2 offers the merge alternative.)
10. **Delivery is two pull requests under this one ADR and one plan.**
    **PR 1** authors the seven building-subsystem classes — §1–§7, 109 rows,
    106 distinct codes, 100 new — under `facility` and `environment`, and adds
    the `facility` domain seed row. **PR 2** authors vertical transport —
    §8a/§8b, 118 rows, 107 distinct, 99 new — under `mechanical`, and depends
    on PR 1 only for `controller_comms_ok`. Each half is close to `E5.2`'s
    measured 107 codes, which is the largest this track has reviewed in one
    sitting. One scope gate (this ADR), one plan ordering both, **two merge
    gates**; the `E5.3` row flips ✅ only when PR 2 merges. The seam is the
    domain boundary and the tag list's own section break, not an arbitrary cut.
    (Gate question 3 offers the single-PR alternative.)
11. **Scope fence.** This ADR authors *content* — vocabulary, entries, alarm
    philosophy rows, derived formulas, maintenance plans — plus one vocabulary
    row. It does **not** build the estate-wide data-quality surface (decision
    6), does not wire alarms to rules (`E2.4`), does not build or rebind
    dashboards (`F3.1`, `F3.45`), does not touch the hierarchy (`F2.10`), does
    not add an alarm skill (`F4.78`), the runtime import parse (`F2.16`) or the
    catalog accordion (`F2.17`), does not model any **command** — no car calls,
    no fire recall, no door release, no lighting override, which the tag list
    itself fences as observe-only — and does not change `apps/sim`.

## Dependencies

**None.** No npm package. No DDL: the `facility` domain is one `INSERT` into an
existing lookup table through the seed path ADR 0031 A1.1 prescribes and `E5.2`
built, and the 199 point keys ride ADR 0051's seed exactly as the water pack's
98 and the mechanical pack's 107 did. AGENTS.md §9.4 is not engaged.
`packages/db` is touched (the seed), so `migration-reviewer` joins the review
on both pull requests.

## Consequences

- **The catalog grows 18 → 25 entries at PR 1 and 25 → 27 at PR 2**, across
  three domain groups in the `F2.17` card, one of them (`facility`) new.
- **The §4.5 line cap is not the pressure `E5.2` faced, and the reason matters.**
  `stock-transcription.spec.ts` (703 lines) is *pack-neutral helpers* and grows
  only if a helper widens, not per entry; the per-pack class specs are already
  split three ways per pack (`water-classes` 1–3, `mechanical-classes` 1–3), so
  `E5.3` authors `facility-classes` 1–n split from the start rather than
  splitting an existing file. The one file under real pressure is
  `stock-catalog.spec.ts` at **840 lines** — the `PACK_SOURCE_DOC` re-key,
  its comment and two new prefixes land there. The plan measures it before
  Task 1 and splits only if the cap is actually crossed; `electrical-classes.spec.ts`
  at 952 is the closest to the cap in the directory and `E5.3` does not touch it.
- **`UNKNOWN_PACK_FIXTURE` needs no move.** It is already
  `undeclared-f213-spec-pack` — `E5.2` foresaw `facility` and moved it off a
  real prefix so this pack would not turn a negative fixture into a positive one.
- **Guard bounds move to their measured actuals**, never left slack:
  `tests/f2.13`, `tests/f3.38`, `tests/f3.39` and `STOCK_ASSET_RELS` each to
  whatever the build measures, twice — once per pull request.
- **The exposure is the extended tier** — 159 of 210 codes, and for facility
  subsystems that means "depends on which gateway the site has", not "depends
  on a sensor being fitted". A dry-contact lift uses eleven of the lift entry's
  seventy-eight rows. This is correctable by one redline session, and it is the
  reason every entry ships `stockVersion: 1` and provisional.
- **Nothing in this pack arrives over MQTT today.** `apps/sim` and
  `apps/ingest` are untouched and stay untouched; the pack is catalog content a
  global administrator imports.
- On acceptance, `E5.3`'s BACKLOG row flips to 🟡 and §5's *Tag-list citation*
  row records `E5.3` as cited — a `docs(backlog):` change on the PR 1 branch,
  the way `E5.1`'s and `E5.2`'s were; the `chore(agents):` sweep at closure is
  separate (§9.10) and waits for PR 2.

## Open questions for the gate — all three answered 2026-09-03

1. **Three domains, or one `facility` for all nine?** Drafted (decision 2) as
   `facility` for six, `environment` for the IAQ node and `mechanical` for the
   lift and escalator, prefix = domain. The alternative is one `facility`
   domain for all nine — simpler to count and one accordion group, but it files
   an IAQ node under a domain other than the one `temperature_c` and
   `humidity_pct` are seeded in, and a lift under a domain other than
   `motor_temp_c`'s. A third alternative, one new row per subsystem
   (`lighting`, `fire`, `security`, `parking`, `vertical_transport`), was
   offered and is the most precise taxonomy, but it breaks the one-new-row-per-
   pack precedent `E5.2` set. **Ruled: three domains, as drafted.**
2. **Lift and escalator as two entries, or one vertical-transport entry?**
   Drafted (decision 9) as two, matching the tag list's own 8a/8b split and
   ADR 0053's ruling that the VFD is its own template. The alternative is a
   single entry carrying the union of 118 rows with the inapplicable ones
   marked in their descriptions — fewer templates, but a global administrator
   provisioning an escalator would see seventy-eight lift points that do not
   apply, which is the opposite of what the tier system exists for.
   **Ruled: two entries, nine classes.**
3. **Two pull requests, or one?** Drafted (decision 10) as two under one ADR
   and one plan, cut at the domain boundary and the tag list's §7/§8 break. The
   alternative is one branch in the shape `E5.1` and `E5.2` shipped — simplest
   provenance, the whole pack atomic, one closure row — at the cost of roughly
   sixteen thousand insertions in a single review. A third alternative, two
   pull requests **and** a board split into `E5.3` plus a new `E5.4` row, was
   offered and declined: it renumbers the Track B tail for a delivery decision
   the plan can carry. **Ruled: two pull requests, one ADR, one plan, one
   backlog row.**
