Step-3 plan (plan-architect, Fable, 2026-11-02). Scope: [ADR 0108 Amendment 4](../adr/0108-trend-and-energy-views.md), merged in #978 (`89c36f1b`) — the §10 approval. ## Owner answers (2026-11-02)

1. **A third site section at `/control-room/site/:locationId/trends`**, for every view kind, with **migration `0119`** reserving `trends` in the `dashboard_tabs` CHECK (item 1).
2. **A segment is an `assets.domain` present at the site** — the distinct domains of the readable assets, in catalog order (item 2).
3. **Per segment: one point at a time**, a chart of the top 8 assets, a ranking of up to 50, **and a per-point abnormal list** (items 4–5).
4. **Energy sub-tab composes `energy/view` + `organization-views` with `locationIds=[site]`; one new segment route** `GET /control-room/sites/:locationId/segment-views` (items 3–4).
5. **Not on the wall** (item 9).
6. **The site ranking shows only when `sites.totalSites > 1`** (item 3).
7. **The abnormal rule**: baseline = median of the asset's mean over the four Amendment 1 item 7 windows; `above` at `ratio ≥ ABNORMAL_RATIO` (1.5); **`below` at `ratio ≤ ABNORMAL_BELOW_RATIO` (0.5, new)**; `not_comparable` at a near-zero baseline (`ABNORMAL_NEAR_ZERO_FRACTION` = 0.01, relative) or opposite signs; `not_enough_history` as F4.299; `normal` counted, not sent; advisory only (item 5).

## 1. Goal

The site page gains **Trends & Energy**, a third "Site sections" entry (`site-page.tsx:242-261`). Inside it, **Energy** shows the `F4.298` KPI tiles, the source-mix load profile, the top consuming assets, the heatmap and the energy abnormal list for this one site (the site ranking only above one site); then **one sub-tab per segment present at the site** shows, for one trend point at a time, a chart of the top 8 assets (compare dashed), a ranking of up to 50 assets by mean or max with the change, and a two-sided advisory abnormal list — over the seven energy ranges with a custom from/to and a compare, in the site's zone, with one rollup level shown. A new segment, point, organization or site needs no release. Every click lands on `/asset-history/:assetId?from=&to=&points=`.

**Out of scope.** The view actions (`F4.303`), the wizard (`F4.306`), kWh per m² (`F4.304`); any change to the strip, the widget, the live tiles, the mimic, the SLD, `SmocSiteView`, `SiteDashboardView`, `GeneratedSiteView`; any change to `energy/view`, `organization-views`, the energy abnormal rule or its one-sided verdict; a heatmap per segment; the wall; any grant, RLS or dependency change.
