## 0. Goal, and what is out

**Goal.** The mimic editor and the mimic widget serve every asset domain, not only water. `mimicSymbolSchema` grows from 12 to 29 members (ADR 0082 decision 1) and migration `0089` restates the list in `mimic_layout_nodes_symbol_check` and inserts 18 `bms.asset_roles` codes (decision 4). Each new symbol has a glyph and a label; the palette shows the 29 in eight groups and the inspector's Symbol select uses the same groups (decision 2). `mimicPresetSchema` grows from `water_train` to seven presets, each a topology in `packages/shared/src/mimic-presets.ts` and coordinates, panels and glyphs in `apps/web/src/lib/mimic.ts`; a preset's `sink` becomes optional and a preset with no pipes is valid (decision 3). The library page replaces the one "Start from Water train" link with a "Start from" select over all seven presets, and the editor's `?preset=` accepts every preset (decision 5). Every existing widget keeps `{ source: "preset", preset: "water_train" }` and draws pixel-identically.

**Out (ADR 0082 decisions 6, 7).** Any seed change; a stored symbol group; connector styles per domain; a `panel` symbol; breaker state, energised paths, bus topology (network mimics); the "Pipe mode" rename; any `AGENTS.md` §6 change; layouts in templates. Also out: any change to the API layout routes, the resolver, or `MimicScene` — they are generic over the two enums already (§1).
