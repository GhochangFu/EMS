## 1. Goal and out of scope

**Goal.** Every `<button>` under `apps/web/src` whose `disabled` expression
references a pending flag announces the pending state: its accessible name
changes while the flag is true (a pending label, or a pending `aria-label`
where an `aria-label` names the button) and it carries
`aria-busy={<that flag>}`. A button disabled only because a sibling action
pends is a declared bystander and keeps its name. A `tests/` gate reddens on a
new site that breaks either rule, keyed on the `disabled` expression, with
derived flags (`const busy = a.isPending || b.isPending`) resolved through the
file.

**Out of scope.** Disabled `<select>` / `<textarea>` / `<input>` fields (7
sites). Changing which buttons are disabled, or when. `aria-pressed` on the
rule toggle. The `disabled:opacity-*` palette (F4.164 D4). Anything under
`packages/*`, `apps/api`, `apps/ingest`. `AGENTS.md` §5 wording (a later
`chore(agents):` PR).
