## 1. Goal

Author the six water-treatment plant classes of `docs/e5.1-derived-taglist-v1.md` as six more `StockAssetTemplateEntry` values, in a new **water pack**, so that every organization can import a real, complete plant template through the mechanism `F2.13` built and `F2.12` proved a second time. Concretely, three things land:

1. **A vocabulary.** `WATER_CLASS_POINT_KEYS` — **98 codes** in `packages/shared/src/constants.ts` (91 table codes + 7 promoted derived codes), 98 explicit `UNIT_BY_KEY` entries in `packages/db/src/point-keys-seed.ts`, one `keysForDomain(…, "water")` line appended **last** to `GLOBAL_CATALOG`, one `ARRAY_DOMAIN` entry, and four guard bounds moved to their new actuals. `bms.point_keys` goes **193 → 291** on a cold start. This is `F2.11`'s shape at two-thirds the size, and it is the **first array filed under a domain other than `electrical`/`hvac`/`it`/`environment`**.
2. **Six entries.** `water-stp`, `water-etp`, `water-cooling-tower`, `water-wtp`, `water-ro`, `water-softener`, authored in **that order** (ADR 0040 ruling 2, the `A3a` order), each declaring its section's table rows as template points (`C` → required + `meta.tier: "core"`, `X` → optional + `"extended"`, `M` → optional + `"manual"`), carrying its alarm bullets as **pair-absent philosophy rows with the ADR 0019 §3 `philosophy` object populated** (ADR 0040 decision 4 — this is the first stock content anywhere to carry one), carrying **maintenance plans** as `content.maintenance`, and carrying **no `content.kpis` at all** for the structural reason in §5.
3. **Seven derived promotions.** Seven of the tag list's 21 named derived codes are genuinely expressible over measured siblings inside their own entry; those are promoted into the array and authored as `kind: "derived"` template points with real formulas (8 point instances — `recovery_pct` is authored twice, on the WTP and the RO). The other **fourteen are deferred and named, never placeholdered**.

When this lands, Ion Exchange's core business is a product artifact rather than a document: `F3.2` has authored content for six plant classes to draw a default dashboard from, and the workshop handout the tag list describes itself as has a product behind it to redline against.

They are v2 work, gated on the same tag-list redline. Say so in the pack docblock so the gap between the row title and the shipped six is a recorded decision, not a silent omission.

- **Every derived code with no formula** — fourteen, each named with its reason in §5 and asserted absent by the build spec. The full ledger is §5.0.
- **`content.kpis`, on every one of the six entries.** §5.0 argues this is a structural consequence of the tag list, not a deferral of effort: every expressible ratio the document names is a *named code an alarm binds*, so it is a point; every ratio that is not is deferred. ## 3. Serial or parallel, and the model per pass

**Serial, one branch, three consecutive `implementer` passes. Do not fan out.**

Six plants *look* like six independent siblings and are not. Every one of them writes the same six shared files — `stock-catalog/water.ts` (the pack index), `stock-catalog/stock-catalog.ts` (its docblock), `stock-catalog/stock-catalog.spec.ts` (`STOCK_ENTRY_CODES`, `DEFERRED_DERIVED_CODES`, the source-document prefix map), `tests/f2.13-asset-stock-catalog-vocabulary.test.ts` (`STOCK_ASSET_RELS` and three bounds, one number per file), `packages/shared/src/constants.ts` and `packages/db/src/point-keys-seed.ts`. §3 of `docs/build-operating-model.md`: *"Anything two agents would edit the same file for — they will conflict."* Six worktrees would produce six conflicting appends to one array and six conflicting edits to one bound, and the merge would be done by hand anyway.

**`E5.1` is not a ⭐ enabler and does not define a seam.** `F2.13` froze `StockAssetTemplateEntry`, the DI token and the two routes; `F2.12` froze the per-class module split, `point-fields.ts` and the generalised `checkEntry`. This row is the **second pack through a mechanism that already carries one**, which is precisely the case ADR 0052's "one module per pack" rule was written for. What it does do is *generalise* three catalog-wide checks from "electrical" to "any pack" (§6 Task 2), and `E5.2`/`E5.3` inherit that generalisation — which is why that task is not Sonnet.

Three passes on `feat/E5.1-water-domain-pack`, each starting only when the previous is green:

| Pass | Tasks | Model | Why, against the §2 ladder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1 — the 98-code array, 98 `UNIT_BY_KEY` entries, one `keysForDomain` line, one `ARRAY_DOMAIN` entry, four bound moves | **Sonnet** | *"A well-specified, self-contained, mechanical unit."* §4.4 supplies every code, every unit spelling and every number. Nothing here is a judgment call. |

| **C** | 4–9 — the six entries, tests-first, one plant per commit, in ADR 0040's ruled order | **Opus** | *"Ordinary multi-file feature work against the plan where judgment is needed in the execution."* 103 point rows, 40 alarms with 40 populated `philosophy` objects, 8 formulas and 23 maintenance plans transcribed from a document, where a wrong tier, a wrong unit or a dropped row is silent until a client's import fails. |

Common to all six: `domain: "water"` (§4.3), `stockVersion: 1`, and a `description` that **cites `docs/e5.1-derived-taglist-v1.md` and its section by name** — ADR 0052 decision 6: the stamp plus the citation is the provenance, and **there is no `meta.provenance`**. **Entry codes and `assetType` values are ruled (§12 ruling 4):** `water-stp`/`stp`, `water-etp`/`etp`, `water-cooling-tower`/`cooling_tower`, `water-wtp`/`wtp`, `water-ro`/`ro_skid`, `water-softener`/`softener`.

Point-row conventions, unchanged from the electrical pack and restated so nobody re-derives them:

- `sourceDataKeyPattern: null` on **every** point (the `MEASURED` spread and `derived()` both supply it). The pattern is the site's telemetry wiring, which the tag list does not know and the catalog must not guess. **The consequence is real and belongs in the pack docblock**: an imported draft cannot be instantiated until an operator fills the patterns in, because a required point with no resolvable key is a 400 (§4.5).
- `unit` is `null` wherever `UNIT_BY_KEY` holds `""` — the eleven `0/1` rows and `cycles_of_concentration`. A template `unit` is an *override*; `null` defers to the catalog's own unit.
- `label` is the Description column with the tag list's editorial remarks dropped (`(lab)`, `(manual)`, `(manual test)`, `*(MBR only)*`, `*(if fitted)*`), and the ISA letter code is **not** carried — nothing reads it and there is no field for it.
- `sortOrder` is the index, and the index is the tag list's own row order. Derived points are appended after the table rows.
- **`meta.tier` is present if and only if `kind === "measured"`**; `core` ↔ `required: true`, `extended` and `manual` ↔ `required: false`. `checkEntry` already enforces both, catalog-wide.
- **The dual-tier tie-break, stated once as a rule rather than per row: the first-listed tier wins.** §5's `effluent_cod_mgl` is `M/X` → `manual`; §6's is `X/M` → `extended`. Both are `required: false` either way, so only `meta.tier` differs — and **the same vocabulary code legitimately carries a different `meta.tier` in two entries**, because `meta.tier` says what *that plant type* typically fits, not what the code is. That is the only place in the pack where it happens, and both entry specs assert it by name.
- Derived points carry `kind: "derived"` via `derived()`, `required: false`, no `meta.tier`, `calcTrigger: "streaming"`, `calcIntervalSeconds: null`, and `maxInputAgeSeconds: null` except the one override in §5.3.

Alarm-row conventions: every alarm is **pair-absent** — no `thresholdValue`, no `operator` — per ADR 0019 Amendment 2 and B7's *"limit values are set per site at commissioning"*. The meaning is carried by `message` (≤ 500 chars) **and, new in this pack, by a populated `philosophy` object** (ADR 0040 decision 4) — `cause`, `impact`, `action`, and `skill` only where one of the five seeded trades genuinely answers (§12 ruling 6: `mechanical` for a pump, blower, fan or press; `electrical` for a motor; `controls` for an analyser or dosing controller; `civil` for a tank, bund or pond; **omitted on the process-chemistry rows**, and the docblock says why). **Discharge-consent parameters carry their CPCB Schedule VI *meaning* in the philosophy text and never a limit value** (ADR 0040 decision 4) — `discharge_ph`, `effluent_cod_mgl`, `effluent_bod_mgl`, `effluent_tss_mgl` and `oil_grease_mgl` are the five, and the ETP's `discharge_ph_out_of_consent` row is the one the tag list calls *"the one regulators check first"*.

### 5.0 The derived-code rule, and the reconciliation ADR 0040 decision 5 forces

**The rule the build follows, in two halves, because the two surfaces have different contracts.**

**(a) Derived *template points*: `bms-calc-v1` only, because nothing else exists.** `templatePointBodySchema.formulaDialect` is `z.literal(CALC_DIALECT).nullish()` and its `superRefine` refuses a `kind: "derived"` point without it. **There is no `unvalidated` derived point.** `templatePointsBodySchema`'s array-level refinement additionally requires every `{ref}` to resolve to a **measured** point the same template declares (ADR 0036 decision 7), and `assertPointKeysActive` checks the output code against `bms.point_keys` where `active = true` — which is why each authored code must be *promoted* and not merely authored. So ADR 0051 Amendment 6 decision 8 applies unchanged: **promote and author exactly the derived codes with an in-entry formula; defer the rest by name, never with a placeholder.**

**(b) `content.kpis`: `bms-calc-v1` only, and `unvalidated` buys nothing.** The `unvalidated` discriminator **does** still exist — `dialect: z.enum(["unvalidated", CALC_DIALECT])` at `asset-templates-content.schema.ts:274`, exactly as ADR 0019 §5 describes. But two shipped facts close it off for a stock entry:

1. **`checkEntry` refuses it, catalog-wide** (`stock-catalog.spec.ts:455-460`, `F2.12` Task 3): *"a stock KPI is always `bms-calc-v1`; `unvalidated` is an affordance for hand-authored content, not for a catalog whose expressions ship to every organization unread."*
2. **Even relaxing that would not make a cross-asset KPI authorable.** `collectContentPointRefs` walks `kpis[].pointKeys` **regardless of dialect** (`…content.schema.ts:736-738`) and feeds `assertContentRefsResolve`, so every key a KPI names must be a key its own entry declares. A KPI that needs another asset's pump kWh cannot name it in either dialect.

**So ADR 0040 decision 5's second half — "cross-asset formulas are authored as `dialect: 'unvalidated'` and promote when `F2.9` lands" — is not implementable in a stock catalog entry, and this row does not implement it.** That is not a question for the owner, because there is no option behind it: deleting the `checkEntry` assertion would be the move `F2.12` §5.6 reason 4 refuses, and it would not achieve the thing anyway. Cross-asset KPIs are **deferred and named**, exactly as `F2.12` deferred `specific_energy_kwh_kl` and `losses_pct`. **The ADR 0040 decision-5 sentence correction is owed in the closure `docs(adr):` commit** (§11), the same channel `F2.13` Task 9 used for its three ADR corrections.

**And the water pack authors no `content.kpis` at all** — which is a structural consequence, not a deferral of effort. Every ratio the tag list names *and* the grammar can express is a **named derived code**, so it becomes a point (an alarm can then bind it, which two of them do); every ratio it names that the grammar cannot express is deferred. `F2.12` invented six KPI codes because the *electrical* tag list named expressible ratios it had no code for. The water tag list has no such gap. **The pack invents no KPI code and no point key.** Each entry spec asserts `content.kpis` is absent, with that reason in the failure message.

## 12. Open questions — all seven ruled by the owner, 2026-09-03

Asked one at a time at the plan gate, each with the plan's recommendation first. The rulings are folded into §§4–6 above; this section is the record.

1. **`pct_recovery` or `recovery_pct`?** The tag list spells it prefix-first; ADR 0040 decision 2's convention is `snake_case` + unit suffix, and every other percentage in the vocabulary is `*_pct`. The code is seeded write-once. The plan-architect recommended the document's spelling to keep the handout and the product identical; the transcriber recommended the convention. **Ruled: `recovery_pct`.** The tag list's `D` rows are corrected in the closure docs PR (§11), and `pct_reuse` is spelled `reuse_pct` in the deferral ledger the same way (no key is created).
2. **`KL/hr` or `kL/h`?** The document's spelling versus SI consistency with `L/hr`. **Ruled: `KL/hr`** — the client's own dashboards show it, and the handout and the product agree at the moment the client reads both.
3. **`pH`, `Hazen`, `SDI15` as named units, or `""`?** **Ruled: keep the three as named units.** ADR 0051 Amendment 6 decision 4 maps only 0/1, enum, code, tap and count rows to the empty string.
4. **Entry codes and `assetType` values.** **Ruled: confirmed as listed** — `water-stp`/`stp`, `water-etp`/`etp`, `water-cooling-tower`/`cooling_tower`, `water-wtp`/`wtp`, `water-ro`/`ro_skid`, `water-softener`/`softener`. `ro_skid` reuses the repository's existing spelling.
5. **The 23 maintenance plans: author them, or ship none?** **Ruled: author them**, marked PROVISIONAL, with the derivation basis in each module docblock (§5.7) — `F2.12` §12 ruling 1 applied to the same gap.
6. **`philosophy.skill` on the process-chemistry alarms.** `bms.alarm_skills` holds `electrical`, `mechanical`, `hvac`, `controls`, `civil` and no process trade. **Ruled: omit `skill` on the process rows**; set it only where one of the five genuinely answers (§5's tables name each). A `process` skill is a new backlog row with its own migration (§11).
7. **`recovery_pct` on the STP and the ETP?** Expressible over declared core points, but the STP's own derived quantity is reuse, a different number. **Ruled: no** — STP and ETP stay at zero derived points, and the docblocks say why.
