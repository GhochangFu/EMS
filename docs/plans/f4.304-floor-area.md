# F4.304 — floor area and kWh per m² (ADR 0108 Amendment 5)

Step-3 plan (plan-architect, Fable, 2026-11-02). Scope: [ADR 0108 Amendment 5](../adr/0108-trend-and-energy-views.md), merged in #981 (`4fe61651`) — the §10 approval. Builds on `F4.298`/`F4.299` (the energy routes and the site ranking), `F4.302` (the site Energy sub-tab, migration `0120`, journal `idx` 121). Code citations are to `main` at `4fe61651`. Row 🟡, P3, not a ⭐ enabler. CodeGraph was not available in this session (no binary, no MCP tool); every citation below was read from the source directly.

## Owner answers (2026-11-02, Amendment 5)

1. **One nullable column** `bms.locations.floor_area_m2 numeric(12,2)` with a CHECK, migration `0121`, journal `idx` 122 (item 1).
2. **Any location may carry an area; a ranked site uses its own only** — no sum of children, no ancestor fallback; no area is "no area", never a zero, never a rank (item 2).
3. **Admin route and form**: `floorArea: { value, unit }` in m² or ft², storage in m²; **an "Area (m²)" column in the Locations list** (item 4).
4. **Onboarding** (draft, workbook, agent tool) carries the same area with a unit; the commit stores m² (item 6).
5. **The ranking**: kWh per m² = the site's kWh **in the selected range** ÷ its own area, not annualised; a new "Rank by: kWh per m²" beside kWh and Peak kW; the same top 50 by kWh, no second server cut (item 7).
6. **The site Energy tile** divides **the site's own assets' kWh**, not the view's Total energy (item 8).
7. **The bounds**: the typed value from 1 to 100 000 000 in either unit (item 3), **and** the converted area at least 1 m² (owner ruling 2026-11-03, ADR 0108 Amendment 5 "Owner rulings 2026-11-03"): 10.77 ft² passes, 10.76 ft² is refused at `floorArea.value`. One refine, `refineFloorAreaM2`, on the HTTP body (`.strict()`) and the onboarding draft (`.strip()`), so the workbook path too.

## 1. Goal

A location can carry a floor area, typed in m² or ft² and stored in m² (`bms.locations.floor_area_m2`, migration `0121`), set in the Locations admin wizard (Place step), shown in the Locations list, and carried by the onboarding draft, workbook and `set_location` tool. The `F4.299` site ranking gains a third sort, kWh per m², computed on the server from the unrounded kWh and the site's own area; sites with no area follow every ranked row and read "no area". The `F4.302` site Energy sub-tab gains a seventh tile, "kWh per m²", on the named site's own assets' kWh over its own area — the same figure as its row in the ranking — with the compare change as its hint. No new route, no new statement on the energy path, no grant, policy, trigger or dependency change.

**Out of scope.** The view actions (`F4.303`); a server-side cut by intensity; an area history (a past range is divided by today's area); summing child areas; the abnormal list, the heatmap, the trend, the segment views, the map, `/auth/me`, the energy preview and its exports, `report-scope.ts`; the copilot `list_locations` read tool; `find_existing`'s location rows; the web onboarding drawer (no location field today); the live tiles, the wall, the strip; any `AGENTS.md` edit (a `chore(agents):` sweep after merge, §9.10).
