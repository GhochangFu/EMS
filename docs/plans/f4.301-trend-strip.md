## 1. Goal

On the Control Room site page (`/control-room/site/:locationId/:tab?`), under the live content of a tab, show a **trend strip**: one section per segment present on the tab, one row per `F4.300` effective trend point of that segment, a range switch 24 h · 7 d · 30 d, and in each row one small sparkline per asset that registers that point (the first six by asset code, "+N more" beyond that). A click on a sparkline opens that asset's history page (`F4.297`) at the same range and point. The strip reads **one new site-scoped batch route**, `GET /control-room/sites/:locationId/trend-strip?range=&assetGroupId=`, which resolves the caller's readable assets once, joins them to the effective trend points of *their own* `assets.domain`, trims the series set to a server bound, and reads every series in one rollup statement. A tab is mapped to segments by **the assets on the tab**, never by a table in code: a `dashboard`-kind tab (the site layout, `F3.73`) narrows to its `assetGroupId`'s members; a tab with no group covers the whole site. A segment the catalog gains later appears with no release; an organization's trend-point override changes the rows with no release; **no growth in segments, organizations or assets can produce a 500** — the server trims (§2 C6).

**Out of scope.** The live tiles, the mimic canvases and the SLD do not change (design non-goal). No Trends tab (`F4.302`), no export/pin/share/schedule (`F4.303`), no compare period on the strip (the history page has it one click away), no custom range, no change to `GET /telemetry/points/:pointRef/aggregate` or to the `F4.300` routes, no new rollup level or continuous aggregate, no site-total line that sums assets (no additivity flag exists in the catalog; ADR 0108 ruling 4.4), no strip on the wall (`?wall=1`), no strip on the six hand-drawn SMOC mimic tabs of `RSMOC-WC` (owner question 3), no URL state for the range (`F4.303`'s share link decides where filters live), no change to the F4.297 route rename itself.
