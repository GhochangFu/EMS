## Goal and out of scope

**Goal.** Every place a user reads the product name — the SPA header, footer, login
page, auth callback, dashboard title and tab title; the report e-mail sentence, the
test-notification subject and body, the OpenAPI and Swagger titles, the onboarding
assistant's prompt; the Keycloak realm display name — reads `IONSiTE NEXUS` instead
of `TRINETRA`. The header and login hero show a two-line text wordmark drawn from
ADR 0078 role tokens; the logo image and both imports are gone. A repository test
holds the rename.

**Out of scope.** A real logo or brand colours (C22b, a later amendment); the D5
identifiers and documents; the header's layout (`F3.29`); any new colour role or
contrast pair; the `chore(agents):` sweep; the post-merge `docs(backlog)` closure;
Keycloak on an existing realm (a deploy step).
