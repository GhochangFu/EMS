## 1. Goal

Six closed colour profiles (`nexus` default, `ocean`, `lagoon`, `indigo`, `graphite`, `high-contrast`). The global admin, or the organization's own admin, chooses one. All users whose home organization it is see every page, dashboard, control room, wall, mimic and chart in that profile. Light/Dark and Neumorphic/Flat stay per user. The layout does not change.

## 2. Out of scope

ADR 0110 *Consequences*: per-user or per-location profiles, a colour picker, a default theme per profile, reports and emails, a live push, a copilot action, "preview as organization". Also: no change to the mimic builder rows `F4.307`–`F4.309` (another session). If U1 must touch a mimic file, it touches only a state colour class and rebases on `main` first.

## 4. The feasibility finding (why OQ1–OQ3 exist)

A scratch checker (not committed) applied draft profile values over the `nexus` tokens and ran the real `TEXT_PAIRS`, `UI_PAIRS`, S1/S2 and the accent ΔE rule. Three facts came out:

1. **An empty allowlist cannot pass.** Seven `nexus` allowlist entries fail because of a **fixed** role or a WCAG exemption, not because of the brand colour: `warning`/`info` dots on `surface`/`canvas` (4), `warning-on-dark` on `surface` (the donut slice), and in dark the two formula-editor selection entries. A profile cannot fix them without changing a fixed role. Two more depend on a changeable role but are hard to fix without a visible redesign: `ink-hint` on `surface` (placeholder, WCAG exempt) and the white toggle knob on `line-strong`. → **OQ1**.
2. **`accent` on `chrome` and `accent` on `surface` cannot both pass 4.5:1 with one value.** `nexus` passes on chrome only because its accent is allowlisted on surface. A dark brand accent (blue, teal, navy) on a dark chrome gives 2.1–2.8:1. One new changeable role for the brand colour on chrome solves it. The OK pill also needs its text colour (`accent-strong` today) as a fixed role, or its pixels change. → **OQ2**.
3. **Blue sits between two fixed state hues.** `info` (sky) and `simulated-ink` (violet) are fixed. A blue or indigo accent near them makes a link look like an info mark or a simulated-value mark. The draft `indigo` (`#4338CA`) was ΔE 6.4 from `simulated-ink`. → **OQ3**.

## 7. Plan rulings (owner, 2026-11-05, one at a time, all as recommended)

ADR 0110 Amendment 1 (in PR D) records them.

- **OQ1 — A.** A new profile may keep **only** the 9 `nexus` allowlist entries whose cause is a fixed role or a WCAG exemption: the 4 `warning`/`info` dots and bars on `surface`/`canvas`, the donut's `warning-on-dark` slice, the placeholder (`ink-hint` on `surface`), the toggle knob (`on-dark` on `line-strong`), and in dark the 2 formula-editor selection entries. Each is re-measured per profile (C6). The 7 brand-green entries never carry over. Refines ADR 0110 Q5.
- **OQ2 — A.** Two more roles beside `ok`: `ok-strong` (fixed, = `nexus` `accent-strong`) and `accent-on-chrome` (changeable, = `nexus` `accent`). 52 roles; no `nexus` pixel moves. Refines ADR 0110 decision 2.
- **OQ3 — A.** A new gate: `accent` ΔE2000 ≥ 12 from `info` and from `simulated-ink`, both themes, every profile. `indigo` is a deep indigo-navy.
- **OQ4 — A.** The `organization_admin` gate is `isOrganizationLevelAdmin` (a direct `user_organization_access` grant), not the home organization. Users still **see** their home organization's profile. Refines ADR 0110 decision 7.
- **OQ5 — A.** Four serial PRs, A → B → C → D, as in §5.
