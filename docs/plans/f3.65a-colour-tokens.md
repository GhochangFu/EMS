## 1. Goal and out of scope

**Goal.** `apps/web` gets a colour vocabulary of role tokens: CSS custom properties in `apps/web/src/index.css` holding space-separated RGB channels under `:root` (light) and `:root[data-theme="dark"]`, each mapped in `apps/web/tailwind.config.js` as `rgb(var(--<role>) / <alpha-value>)` so `/NN` opacity keeps working. An inline script in `apps/web/index.html`, before the module script, reads `localStorage["bms.theme"]` and sets `data-theme` on `<html>` before the first paint; `color-scheme` follows. Three `tests/` gates hold the rule: a contrast test (every declared pair, both themes, exact allowlist), a mapping-table test (every old class → a role; exact rows equal Tailwind's resolved value; merged rows carry their ΔE), and a ratchet source-scan gate (palette classes, hex literals, colour functions, `dark:`; per-file exact floor that may only fall). `layouts/app-shell.tsx` and `pages/login-page.tsx` move onto role classes. The light theme is pixel-identical on every frequent shade. **No visible switch**: dark is reached only by setting the key.

**Out of scope.** Every other component and page (`F3.65b`). Charts, the CRAC/SLD/SMOC/world-map SVGs, `formula-editor.tsx`, `WIDGET_TONE_COLOR`, the login hero's hex art (`F3.65c`). The visible Light/Dark control (`F3.65c`). Removing the stock palette or `bms.*` from `tailwind.config.js` (`F3.65c`, when the gate is at zero). A per-user column or route. `prefers-color-scheme`. PDF/Excel/e-mail, Keycloak pages, the two mockup HTML files. `AGENTS.md` §5 wording (a later `chore(agents):` PR, decision 7).

## 3. Design decisions

- **D1 The mapping key is the class, not the shade.** `white` is a surface under `bg-` and a foreground under `text-`; `red-600` is a fill under `bg-`/`stroke-` and text under `text-`. The table (§2.3) is keyed by utility + shade, with a default role and, where a class serves two contexts (`bg-bms-green-dark`, `text-white`), a listed alternative the migrating hand picks.
- **D2 `on-accent` flips in dark** (white → `#0F1620`), because white on the ruled dark accent `#3DCD58` is 2.09:1 (OQ2). `on-dark` stays white for chrome, status fills and scrims, which are dark in both themes. `accent-strong` is *lighter* than `accent` in dark (`#65D77A`) so a hover still reads as a step and keeps 10:1 under `on-accent`. `chrome-nav` is a separate role so the nav bar stays `#007C3C` under white text (5.32) in both themes.
- **D3 Derivation rule** (decision 6): keep the light value's hue and saturation; raise lightness in 0.5 % steps until the pair reaches 4.5:1 (text) or 3:1 (UI) on sheet, paper, and the role's own dark wash(es). Washes are the fill hue at L 15 % / 20 %, S 45 %; lines at L 30 %, S 40 %. The three critical inks converge in dark (`#E76A6A`, `#E86A6A`, `#EC8585`) — the rule's literal result (OQ6).
- **D4 Ratchet as an exact per-file table**, not a total: a total lets one file rise while another falls. `{ file, palette, hex, func }` must equal the scan exactly, both directions, like `BYSTANDER_ALLOWLIST` in F4.168; the review rule (docblock) is that a number may only fall or a row disappear. `dark:` and named colours have no table: any occurrence is a finding from day one (OQ7).
- **D5 Tailwind `darkMode: ["selector", '[data-theme="dark"]']`** is set so a stray `dark:` class can never follow the OS preference (OQ8); the gate forbids the class itself.
- **D6 The inline script sets `data-theme` always** (`"light"` or `"dark"`), so the stylesheet may rely on the attribute; the `:root` block is the light default regardless. `color-scheme` lives in the stylesheet (`:root { color-scheme: light }`, dark block `color-scheme: dark`). `nginx.conf` sets no CSP; if one is added later the script needs a hash — the script comment says so.
- **D7 No `lib/theme.ts` yet.** `F3.65c` adds the writer and a single-source test binding its key literal to the script's. Here the literal `bms.theme` lives in `index.html` and its test.
- **D8 The stock palette and `bms.*` stay in `tailwind.config.js`** until the gate is at zero (`F3.65c` deletes them).
