## 1. Goal

`GET /api/v1/mimic-layouts/:id/preview?assetGroupId=<uuid>` answers the
`mimic-nodes` shape (`dashboardMimicNodesResponseSchema`): one
`source: "layout"` widget, its resolved nodes and its `stateMaps`, for the
stored layout bound to one asset group. The resolver is split so the dashboard
read and the preview share statements 1b–4; only statement 1 is replaced by one
synthetic widget. Nothing is written; no audit row.

In the editor, "Preview with site": pick a site, then an asset group of that
site. The canvas draws the saved layout with resolved nodes and live values,
read-only. Selecting a roled unit makes the inspector list the bound asset
(code and name), its shown points, its state points and its open-alarm count,
or "No asset at this site". With unsaved edits the preview says "Save to
refresh the preview".

**Out of scope** (decision 7): a preset preview, an unsaved-edits preview, a
preview in the dashboard builder, any write; any change to the dashboard read's
answer, the socket gateway, `index.css` or `AGENTS.md`.

## 9. Open questions for the owner

1. **`dashboardId` and `widgetId` in the preview answer** (the shared schema
   needs a UUID in both). (a) The layout id in both, documented; (b) the nil
   UUID. *Recommended: (a).*
2. **Which sites the picker lists.** (a) Every active location of the layout's
   organization, "No asset group at this site" when empty; (b) only locations
   that hold an asset group. *Recommended: (a).*
3. **A fan-out unit in the inspector.** (a) The shown (first) asset plus the
   `+N` badge; (b) every member up to `MIMIC_FANOUT_MAX`. *Recommended: (a).*
4. **How `MimicLayoutsModule` reaches the resolver.** (a) Provide its own
   stateless `MimicNodesService`; (b) import `DashboardBuilderModule`.
   *Recommended: (a).*
