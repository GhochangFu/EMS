## 1. Goal

Under the `F4.298` filter on `/energy` (organization, locations, range, custom from/to, compare), add four organization views, all from the readable assets only and all at the one level, bucket and zone the `F4.298` view already resolved: (1) **sites ranked** by kWh and by peak kW (with its time), with the previous-period figure and change when compare is on; (2) the **total trend stacked by site or by source**, the compare period's total as a dashed line; (3) a **day × hour heatmap** of kWh in the view's zone, DST days included; (4) an **abnormal list** of sites and assets whose kWh over the range is far above their own baseline, with "not enough history" listed as such — advisory only, never an alarm or a notification. One new read route carries the four sections; the page issues it beside `GET /reports/energy/view` and renders both. Every list the server sends is bounded (§2 C11), so a client with many sites or assets cannot produce a 500 or an unbounded body.

**Out of scope.** kWh per m² and floor area (`F4.304`). Export, pin, share link, schedule (`F4.303`). The Control Room Trends tab (`F4.302`; it will call the same two routes with `locationIds=[site]`). Forecasting (`E1.4`), any ML or seasonal model, a "below normal" list, any alarm, rule or notification from the abnormal verdict. Any change to `GET /reports/energy/preview`, the exports, the PDF, the copilot tools, or `energyViewQuerySchema`'s fields. A new rollup level or retention change. Browser-local time anywhere. A location roll-up (a parent's row summing its children): the tree is flat today (depth 1, measured); a site is the asset's own `location_id`.

| C7 | **Abnormal statistic** (owner question Q1 for the thresholds). Entities: every site and every readable asset **with a `kw` row in the current period** (an entity with none is not judged and not listed). `current` = the entity's **kWh** over the current period (Σ buckets × `bucketHours`, the C4 definition — a site is its summed buckets, not Σ of asset means). Baseline windows: `BASELINE_WINDOWS = 4` copies of the current period shifted back by `k × step` local days, `k = 1..4`, `step = ceil(durationHours / 24)` for **every** range kind (`this_month` included — a 15-day month-to-date compares with the four preceding 15-day blocks). Shift = `shiftByLocalDays(instant, −k·step, zone)` = `toInstant(addLocalDays(localDateOf(instant)), {hour, minute} of wallTimeOf(instant), zone)` **plus the sub-minute remainder `instant.getTime() % 60_000`**, so `now`'s seconds survive (`toInstant` takes hh:mm only; `wallTimeOf` is exported for it — one declaration, `report-period.ts:113`). A window **counts** when the entity's distinct read-bucket count in it is ≥ half its distinct bucket count in the current period (`count(DISTINCT bucket)` for both assets and sites). `baseline` = median of the counting windows' kWh. Verdict: `not_enough_history` with fewer than `MIN_BASELINE_WINDOWS = 3` counting windows or `baseline === 0`; `above` when `current / baseline ≥ ABNORMAL_RATIO = 1.5`; else `normal`. **Sent:** `above` entries (ratio desc) then `not_enough_history` entries (name asc), **capped at `ABNORMAL_LIST_MAX = 100`** with `truncated: boolean`; `normal` entities are **counted, not sent** (`normalCount: { sites, assets }`).
