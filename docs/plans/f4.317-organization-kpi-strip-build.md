## 1. Goal

The organization level of the Control Room (`/control-room/org/:organizationId`) shows a 14-tile KPI strip at the top, scoped to that organization and to the caller's readable assets. The strip comes from the template system: a stock `organization`-target template (`organization-standard`) is drawn with no row for every organization (the fail-safe), and an admin — global, organization, or a `location_admin` of that organization — can "Make organization layout" (copy the published template into an organization-wide dashboard, write a pointer row in one transaction), edit that dashboard in the builder, or "Reset to default". The metric catalog gains `sites.live.count`, `pue.ratio` and `alarms.critical.count` (ruling Q1b, ADR 0112 Amendment 1 — there is no `tone` param); a catalog resolve gains a 24 h prior for a tile that asks `compareToPrevious`. The estate "Sites online" tile moves to the location count.

**Out of scope** (ADR 0112 decision 1 and Consequences): the whole organization page from a template; the estate strip's other three tiles; widget types other than `value_tile` on the `organization` target; a "worst site" dataset; one total-cost tile over two point keys; `tabs` on an organization template; a target picker in the template create form (today's form has no target input — `apps/web/src/pages/admin/dashboard-templates-page.tsx:219` — and the stock import is the path the ADR names); the site level.
