## Goal

Three serial pull requests, then a closure PR. **PR 1** adds the per-asset balance role: migration `0080` (`bms.water_balance_roles` in the `0051` shape, seeded `intake`/`discharge`/`reuse`/`internal`, and the nullable `bms.assets.water_balance_role` column), the seventh vocabulary on `GET /api/v1/vocabularies`, the field on the asset write path and the asset form, and the optional `balanceRole` field on `sustainability.total` / `sustainability.by_location` that narrows the carrying set (and its coverage) to assets with that role. **PR 2** adds the balance itself: `outlet_kl_today/_this_month/_this_year` on the water classes (v5), migration `0081` widening `catalog_key`'s CHECK for the new dataset entry `water.balance` (`{ period }` → one row per site: Intake, Reuse, Discharge, Consumed or lost = Intake − Discharge, Coverage), and `sustainability-overview` v4 (`balanceRole: "intake"` on the water bindings, plus the *Water balance by site* table). **PR 3** seeds a demo water plant (WTP intake, RO internal, cooling tower internal, STP reuse, ETP discharge) at one ESKOM location on seed-side mirror templates, makes `apps/sim` emit its flows, and raises `verifyHierarchySeed`'s counts.

**Out of scope** (ADR 0073): any formula for `water_recycle_pct` (decision 5 — the draft goes to the client through B14, nothing computes it); a fifth role code or an admin CRUD route for the vocabulary (decision 1); split flows / asset topology (`F2.10`); a params editor in the builder (E4.2 OQ3 stands — `water.balance` is hidden from the picker like its siblings); a label caveat on v3 (Q1 ruling); new dependencies; the `chore(agents):` sweep (separate PR, §9.10).

## Owner rulings on the PR 2 review (2026-09-23)
