## 1. Goal

An administrator chooses, per trend strip widget, which segments and which points (at most 30, each on its segment's `F4.300` list) the strip draws, and a series colour per point from eight new theme roles. The strip route reads the selection through two new query parameters. A wizard writes such widgets from the builder (draft only) or from the site dashboard view (through a `trend_strip` arm of the append route), replacing a tab's existing strip or adding one at the tab's foot. A config without the new fields is exactly today's config.

Out of scope (Amendment 7 item 8): the strip DTO shape, the fixed strips, the stock layout and its version, the seed chain, every migration, the `F4.300` lists and their admin, the strip export routes (they follow), the chart arm of the append route, `SERIES_PALETTE`, the copilot catalog and tool registry, a per-tab selection in one wizard run.

## 2. Two PRs, serial (owner, OQ6)
