## Owner answers (2026-11-03, Amendment 6)

1. **Two PRs, one amendment** (Q1): PR 1 = Link + Export (no migration, no write route); PR 2 = Pin + Schedule (migration `0122`, the append route, the schedule body change). PR 2 branches from `main` after PR 1 merges and changes no rule PR 1 sets.
2. **Link** (Q2): every piece of view state in the URL, ids and codes only; a "Copy link" button copies `location.href`; the reader's own access is checked at open.
3. **Export** (Q3): server-built CSV and XLSX of the bucketed data, 50 000 rows (`MAX_EXPORT_ROWS`, imported) over the view's own range, a 400 above the cap, checked before any `telemetry.*` statement.
4. **Pin** (Q4, Q10): point views only (asset history, a segment sub-tab), one trailing-window `chart` widget with `aggregate` set, appended at the foot of a chosen tab; the widget ladder's level is accepted.
5. **The append route** (Q5): `POST /dashboards/:id/widgets/append`.
6. **Schedule** (Q6, Q7): the existing wizard and `POST /reports/schedules`; `energy_consumption` for the Energy views, a new `trend_points` template for the point views; `bms.report_schedules.params jsonb` (migration `0122`); each run reports the cadence period that just ended.
7. **Export only on the trend strip** (Q8); no Link, Pin or Schedule there.
8. **Compare on an Energy view schedule** (Q9): the `energy_consumption` PDF and XLSX gain a previous-period column; without compare the bytes do not change.

## 1. Goal

Each of the five trend views gains the actions of the matrix in Amendment 6 item 1. **PR 1:** the Energy page and the site Trends & Energy section hold their whole state in the URL (`view-links.ts`), every view with Link shows a "Copy link" button, and eight new `export.csv` / `export.xlsx` routes — beside `energy/view`, `segment-views`, `trend-strip` and a new `assets/:assetId/history` pair — answer the screen's own query with long-format rows through the F4.29 guard, capped at `MAX_EXPORT_ROWS` before the first telemetry statement. `QUERY_SCHEMAS` 31 → 32, moved first into a sibling file because the ledger spec is at 999 lines. **PR 2:** `POST /dashboards/:id/widgets/append` adds one `chart` widget at the foot of a tab inside one tenant transaction that stamps `updated_at`; `bms.report_schedules.params` (migration `0122`, journal `idx` 123) carries `{ compare }` for an `energy_consumption` schedule and `{ points, compare }` for the new `trend_points` template; the render branches on the stored template, the energy report gains `Previous` and `Change` columns when compare is on, and a `trend_points` run writes one PDF and XLSX of per-point bucket tables, naming dropped points.

**Out of scope.** A PDF view export; a Link, Pin or Schedule on the strip; Pin on the Energy views; a `PATCH` that changes a schedule's template or points; `If-Match` on the append; a `template_id` CHECK; any change to the view reads, their response contracts, `chartConfigSchema`, `PUT :id/widgets`, the energy preview and its three `energy/export.*` routes, the 31-day `energyReportRange`, the dispatch tick, the queue payload, `bms.report_files`, the copilot preview tool, the abnormal rules, the wall; auditing an export; any `AGENTS.md` edit (a `chore(agents):` sweep after each merge, §9.10).
