## 1. Goal, and what is out of scope

**Goal.** `apps/api/src/admin/dashboard-templates/stock-catalog.ts` binds four point keys that
exist in no `*_POINT_KEYS` array — `flow_rate` (lines 200, 231, 310), `dissolved_oxygen` (329,
352), `ph` (432), `cod` (446, 460) — so the resolver's exact-match lookup
(`dashboard-templates-instantiate.service.ts:343-356`, keyed `${assetId}::${pointKey}`) can
never find them and every one of those widgets reports `unresolved` for every organization.
After this row the eight sites bind the six `E5.1` codes the owner ruled, the two `m3/h` labels
read `KL/hr` (the seeded unit), `tests/f3.38-stock-catalog-vocabulary.test.ts` carries the four
keys unconditionally because the allowlist that exempted them is gone, the catalog docblock
records the role reading per binding and the v1 one-role-per-plant consequence, and one
integration case proves a rebound `stp-overview` widget resolves `bound` against a real
`aeration` member carrying a real `aeration_do_mgl` point.

**Explicitly out of scope.**

- **No change to any `assetRoleCode`.** All six roles at the eight sites are `0051` rows
  (`packages/db/drizzle/0051_asset_role_vocabulary.sql:139,140,145,147,152,153`) and
  `f3.38`'s role it() (`:409-421`) already holds them green.
- **No demo fix.** `demoRoleForAsset` returns `null` for every non-electrical domain
  (`packages/db/src/asset-groups-seed.ts:99-102`) and the seed creates no water asset, no
  water membership and no water point (`"water"` appears in `packages/db/src/*seed*.ts` only
  at `point-keys-seed.ts:817`, the vocabulary line). After this row every water/STP/ETP stock
  widget still reports `unresolved` **in seeded data**. §7 files this as a candidate row.
- **No tank/gauge widgets** for the water entries (`stock-catalog.ts:110-130` records that gap
  as its own content change).
- **No `stockVersion` bump.** The entries were never resolvable, so no organization holds a
  working v1 to be superseded; the `F3.2` precedent (no bump for a stock correction) applies.
- **No change to ADR 0051's text** at `:142-143` and `:851-853`. Both sentences were true when
  written ("*will be seen to land*", "*is untouched* — checked by set intersection") and are the
  ADR's record of its own commit, not a present-tense claim. Recommendation: leave; see Q4.
- **The `docs/BACKLOG.md` closure row and the `docs/roadmap.md:5367` mirror** — the caller's
  separate `chore(agents):` / `docs(backlog):` commit (§9.10), never this branch's feature
  commits. `AGENTS.md` names none of the four keys and not `F3.45`, so no §6 softening is owed.
